# Privacy Policy — Moneta

*Last updated: 2026-08-24*

Moneta is a personal finance app. This policy explains what data it handles and how.

## Data storage

All your data — transactions, categories, bills, budgets, accounts, goals — is stored **locally on your device**. Moneta does not run its own servers and does not send your financial data to any backend operated by us.

## Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, Moneta requests the `drive.file` OAuth scope. This scope only lets the app access files it creates itself — it cannot see, read, or modify any other file in your Drive. Moneta uses this solely to store one backup/sync file (`moneta-sync.json`) containing your transactions, categories, bills, budgets, accounts, and goals, so you can restore or sync that data across your own devices.

This sync is manual ("Sync now") plus one automatic pull on app startup — Moneta does not sync continuously in the background. You can disconnect Google Drive access at any time from your [Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), which revokes the app's access; your local data is unaffected.

## Third parties

Moneta does not use analytics, ads, trackers, or crash-reporting services. The only third-party service it talks to is the Google Drive API, and only when you explicitly enable sync.

## Data deletion

Since your data lives on your device, uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it. If you synced to Google Drive, deleting `moneta-sync.json` from the "Moneta" folder in your Drive (or revoking the app's Drive access) removes the remote copy.
